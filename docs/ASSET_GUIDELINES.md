# Image asset and illustration guidelines

The repository currently contains the brand logo but no approved maternal-health photography library. Do not fill this gap with fabricated patient photographs, generic stock presented as local, or images that imply a real patient story.

## Approval checklist

1. Confirm written permission, licence scope, intended use and any model-release requirements.
2. Prefer authentic Nigerian maternal-care contexts where the people and setting are represented respectfully.
3. Avoid identifiable patient records, clinical screens, hospital wristbands or other personal information.
4. Do not imply a person is a patient, healthcare professional, partner or testimonial without documented verification and consent.
5. Record source, licence, permission owner, date approved and expiry/review date in the asset register.
6. Provide concise alt text for informative images; use empty alt text for decorative imagery.
7. Export appropriately sized AVIF/WebP assets, preserve a sensible fallback, and use Next.js image optimisation.
8. Check crop, contrast and legibility at mobile widths and with text enlargement.

## Interim treatment

Until approved imagery is available, use the existing brand mark, clear iconography and simple explanatory diagrams. Mark any future image slot as awaiting approved photography rather than showing a misleading placeholder.
